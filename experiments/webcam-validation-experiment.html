Step 1 of 7 - Setup
Start the camera

Press Enable camera below and choose Allow when your browser asks. The checklist is recommended setup only, it does not block the camera button. If no prompt appears, read the diagnostics under the button: they show whether the address, the embedding frame, or a saved site block is stopping the request.

3
  • Camera at eye level. The lens should be roughly level with your eyes, not looking up at you from the desk.
  • Fixed position. Laptop stays put for the whole session. Do not move it between calibration and trials.
  • Good lighting. Face and shoulders evenly lit from the front or side.
  • No backlight. No bright window or lamp behind you; a backlit silhouette breaks landmark tracking.
  • Shoulders in frame. Sit at your normal working distance so head and both shoulders are visible.

Tap each item to confirm it as you check your setup. The camera button works either way.
