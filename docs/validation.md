# Validation gate (pending measurement)

The PRD requires a fixed-camera experiment on the development laptop before
the reminder flow is considered supported: calibrate comfortably upright,
hold a head tilt and then a shoulder tilt for six seconds each (returning to
reference between attempts), repeat five times per cue, then test five
minutes of ordinary typing, looking around, and brief reaching, plus
deliberate loss of required landmarks.

Each cue proceeds only if it detects at least four of five deliberate holds,
produces no reminders during the ordinary-work sample, and pauses when
required landmarks disappear. Angle thresholds, visibility cutoffs, and
smoothing settings will be recorded here with the measured results once the
experiment runs.
